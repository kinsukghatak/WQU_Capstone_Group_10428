# WQU_Capstone_Group_10428: 
### This repo contains the codes for the data prep, EDA and modelling for the WQU - Capstone project for Group : 10428
### Clustering and Correlation Dynamics in Global Stock and Sectoral Indices Before and After COVID-19

#### Project Overview:
- Institution: WorldQuant University (WQU) Master of Science in Financial Engineering
- Group: Team 10428
- Focus: Analyzing the impact of COVID-19 pandemic on stock market behavior and risk-return profiles for the developed and emerging economies
- Methodology: Exploratory analysis, Statistical analysis, regime detection,Volatility modeling and clustering techniques

### Research Objectives:
●	Study the correlation and co-movement relationship between the selected indices before and after the pandemic.
●	Investigate the associated sectorial indices of the selected indices to understand the relationship between the key sectors of the economy. We will primarily focus on sectors like healthcare, financials, manufacturing and technology.
●	Along with the clustering technique in place, we will try to see if groups or segments can be formed using the sub sectoral index returns.
●	Again, from the selected stocks we will examine the direction and level of spillovers to properly understand investors actions during the pandemic. 


We have performed the following tasks and analysis in sequence: 
- Exatract and process stock market indices data by conencting to Yahoo Finance
- Perform extensive EDA on the data by studying the correlation and co-movement relationship between the selected indices before and after the Covid-19 pandemic.
- Perform regime change analysis and detailed statistical distribution analysis ,correlation analysis, Fisher Z test etc. to draw conclusions based on behaviour , trends and returns from the key indices.
- Perform GARCH(1,1) volatilty modelling on the 8 indices to understand the change in volatility pattern in the post pandemic era compared to the pre pandemic time. 
- Investigate 16 sectoral indices and ETFs and perform a clustering using k-means to analyse the pre and post pandemic change in risk-return profile.
